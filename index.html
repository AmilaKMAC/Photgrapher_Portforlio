[ ABOUT ]

Ten years behind the camera, still chasing good light.

I picked up a camera at eighteen and never really put it down. These days I split my time between wedding days, studio portrait sessions, live events and commercial shoots for small businesses who need photos that actually look like their product, their food, their space — not a stock photo stand-in.

Based in Colombo, available to travel for weddings and destination shoots. I shoot on a mix of Canon and Sony bodies, edit everything myself, and turn galleries around fast so you're not waiting months to relive the day.
